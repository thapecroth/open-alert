FIELD MANUAL

OPEN ALERT — new commander orientation. Read this once, stop losing to easy bots.

THE GOLDEN RULES

  • Economy beats everything. Whoever harvests more ore wins the late game. Build harvesters early, expand to new ore patches, protect them.
  • Power is life. Low power disables your defenses, radar and superweapon. Watch the power meter — if it goes red, build a Reactor Plant immediately.
  • Structures and defenses build in PARALLEL. You have two construction queues — a turret never blocks your factory.
  • Scout or die. Fog of war hides everything. A $45 FPV drone or a cheap infantry squad buys you the information that wins fights.

1
Reactor Plant $300 → Refinery $600 + harvesters — same economy start.
2
Naval Shipyard $1000 — must touch water within range of your base. The sea is a second front — own it or lose it.
3
Gunboats first $600 — cheap scouts that clear the way.
4

Water map truth: ships can't cross land — if your shipyard's sea is boxed in by bridges, canals or ice, reposition before you invest.

ADAPT TO YOUR MAP

Chokepoint maps — Crossroads, Canyon, Fortress

One or two lanes decide the game. Wall the choke: turrets + arc towers + a squad of rocket infantry behind them. Then siege with Artillery and HIMARS — never run tanks into a defended lane head-on.

Open maps — Basin, Tundra, Jungle, Oasis, Cybergrid

No safe lane = attack can come from anywhere. Stay mobile: tank groups + supply trucks, radar coverage, interceptors on standby. Speed and flanking beat turtling here.

Huge 8-player theaters — Pacific Theatre, Megacontinent, Arctic Front, Europa, Bering

You can't defend every border — pick a neighbor to pressure and a border to fortify. Expand to secondary ore fields early; long games are won by whoever controls 3+ ore patches.

Strait maps — Hormuz, Bering

The water channel IS the map. Navy controls it, shore artillery contests it, and whoever owns the strait strangles the other side's expansion.

ECONOMY DEEP DIVE

  • 2–3 harvesters per refinery is the sweet spot — they claim separate ore tiles and never jam.
  • Engineers capture Oil Derricks (income) and Coal Mines (+200 power). Neutral structures on the map are free money — grab them before the enemy does.
  • Supply Hub $650 extends your build radius far forward — how you siege and expand.
  • Supply Trucks keep an army in the field: auto-repair damaged vehicles and rearm empty magazines mid-fight.
  • Ore runs out. When your patch depletes, send harvesters to the next field early — idle harvesters are lost games.

CONTROLS & GADGETS

  • A attack-move — the one order that wins fights
  • S stop · G hold position · P patrol
  • B place finished structure · Q sell · R repair
  • Double-tap a unit: enter first-person mode — drive tanks, fly jets, gun from the AC-130
  • PAUSE button (solo vs bots): freeze the sim, plan your next move, resume
  • Click any defense: see its range ring — place for overlapping coverage
  • Radar powers your minimap & recon scan — losing it blinds you
  • Shift in 1P = sprint/boost · 123 = weapon select
